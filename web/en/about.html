About Inseol Saju

Inseol Saju is a free web app that calculates your Korean saju (the Four Pillars of Destiny, also known as BaZi) and your Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star astrology) chart in one place, and explains them in plain English. Readability comes first: everyday words instead of jargon, with the technical terms kept in a "Chart basis" box for anyone who wants them.

It is made by Inseol, a Korean saju practitioner and writer. The name comes from 인생설계, "designing your life". The same calculations power the Korean site at app.inseol.com and our Android app.

What you can see

  • Your Four Pillars: stems and branches, Ten Gods, hidden stems, life stages, strength, useful element, structure, combinations and clashes
  • Element and Ten God balance, 10-year luck cycles with a life graph, year-by-year and month-by-month readings
  • Zi Wei Dou Shu natal, decade and yearly charts: the 14 main stars, helper and friction stars, brightness and the four transformations
  • Plain-English readings for saju, Zi Wei Dou Shu and both combined, plus a calendar and a list of saved charts

How we calculate

  • The year and month pillars change at the solar terms (Start of Spring and the other "jie" terms), checked against your exact moment of birth.
  • For births outside Korea, your birthplace's time zone and daylight saving history come from your browser's time zone database. By default, the hour and day pillars use local solar time based on the city's longitude; you can switch to standard clock time.
  • For births in Korea, the changes to Korea's standard meridian (1954–1961) and Korea's past daylight saving periods are applied.
  • If the adjusted time falls between 11 pm and midnight, the day pillar moves to the next day, since the Rat hour that starts at 11 pm begins the new day.
  • Zi Wei Dou Shu uses the Korean lunar calendar. A leap month is treated as the regular month with the same number.
  • Ages for the Zi Wei Dou Shu decades use East Asian age reckoning, where you are 1 at birth.
