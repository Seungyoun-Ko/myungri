About Inseol Saju

Inseol Saju is a free web app that calculates your Korean saju (Four Pillars of Destiny) and your Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star) chart in one place, and explains them in plain English. Our first priority is readability: everyday words instead of jargon, with the technical terms kept in a "Chart basis" box for anyone who wants them.

It is made by Inseol, a Korean saju practitioner and writer. The name comes from 인생설계, "designing your life". The same calculations power the Korean site at app.inseol.com and our Android app.

What you can see

  • Your Four Pillars: stems and branches, Ten Gods, hidden stems, life stages, strength, useful element, structure, combinations and clashes
  • Element and Ten God balance, 10-year luck cycles with a life graph, year-by-year and month-by-month readings
  • Zi Wei Dou Shu natal, decade and yearly charts: the 14 main stars, helper and friction stars, brightness and the four transformations
  • Plain-English readings for saju, Zi Wei Dou Shu and a combined view, plus a calendar and a saved-charts list

How we calculate

  • Year and month pillars follow the solar terms (Start of Spring and the other "jie" terms), compared against the actual moment of birth.
  • For births outside Korea, your birthplace's time zone and daylight saving history come from your browser's time zone database. By default the hour and day pillars use local solar time based on the city's longitude; you can switch to standard clock time.
  • For births in Korea, Korean standard-meridian changes (1954–1961) and Korean daylight saving periods are applied.
  • If the adjusted time falls in the 11 pm hour, the day pillar moves to the next day (early Rat hour convention).
  • Zi Wei Dou Shu uses the Korean lunar calendar; leap months are placed as the month of the same number.
  • Ages for Zi Wei Dou Shu decades use East Asian age, where you are 1 at birth.
