Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026 · Updated: October 23, 2026

Inseol Saju (the "Service", https://app.inseol.com) respects your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled.

1. Birth details

The name, gender, date, time and place of birth you enter are used for calculation only inside your browser. They are never sent to the operator's server, and the operator does not collect or store them.

2. Browser storage

Saved charts, your last input and your theme setting are kept in your device's browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of them, and deleting a saved chart removes that chart.

3. Advertising and cookies

The Service may show Google AdSense ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • How Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent management platform.

4. Usage statistics

To improve the Service we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies and only counts things like the page address, the referring page, browser and device type, and country. Birth details are never part of a page address, so they are never sent to analytics.

5. KakaoTalk sharing (Korean site)

The Korean site loads Kakao's sharing script only when you tap a KakaoTalk share or invite button. The message contains your day pillar type, a one-line reading and a compatibility invite link with a day pillar code only, never your name or birth date and time. Kakao's privacy policy applies inside KakaoTalk.

6. Sharing with third parties

Because the operator does not collect birth details, they are not shared with anyone.

7. Children

The Service does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email chcgpapa@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its effective date. The Korean version is the original.
