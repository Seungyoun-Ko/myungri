What is saju?

Saju (사주, "four pillars") is the Korean tradition of the Four Pillars of Destiny, known in Chinese as BaZi. The year, month, day and hour of your birth each become a pillar of two characters. The balance of Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water among those eight characters describes your temperament, your strengths and the rhythm of your life in 10-year luck cycles.

What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?

Zi Wei Dou Shu (자미두수 in Korean), or Purple Star astrology, maps the same moment onto twelve palaces, each standing for an area of life such as career, money or relationships. Reading it alongside your saju gives a fuller picture.
